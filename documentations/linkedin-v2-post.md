Assignment Hub v2 is here! 🚀

I built Assignment Hub to help students and lecturers manage courses, assignments, submissions, and grades in one place. For v2, I focused on making that experience smoother, clearer, and more reliable.

Here’s what’s new:

• A refreshed interface across the landing page and dashboards, with a more professional 3D hero illustration and improved mobile layouts.
• A sliding transition between login and sign-up, plus a lightweight 3D book loader and loading placeholders for dashboard content.
• Faster access to public pages: the site now renders while the session check runs in the background.
• Google sign-in, with account-type selection for new users, and email verification for email/password registration.
• More lecturer controls for editing and deleting courses and managing assignments.
• Password-protected course enrollment.
• Persistent file storage through Supabase, improved upload validation, and more reliable mobile authentication.
• Behind-the-scenes improvements including pagination, database indexes, request limits, and automated checks.

One lesson from this update: small details matter. A loading screen, an interrupted transition, or a slow first visit can shape how the whole product feels.

Built with React, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, Prisma, and PostgreSQL.

I’d love feedback from students, lecturers, and fellow developers: what would make managing coursework easier for you?
